# BE Overtime Policy 2026

Applies to: all Belgian employers. Effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Voluntary overtime

Employees may perform voluntary overtime up to a cap of 180 hours per calendar year. The first 120 hours are exempt from social security contributions and withholding tax (net = gross). Hours above 120 are taxed normally.

## 2. Registration

Voluntary overtime requires a written agreement between employer and employee, valid for six months and renewable. The employer registers the hours in the payroll system under code OT-V.

## 3. Sector rules

Joint committees may not lower the 180-hour cap. Sector-specific rules only apply where an annex says so.
